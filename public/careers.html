How We Work

An environment built for
senior engineers

Senior-to-senior work

You'll work alongside architects and engineers with deep enterprise experience. Nobody gets siloed into junior support roles after joining.

Hard problems only

Our clients come to us because the problem is genuinely difficult. You'll work on migrations, AI systems, compliance-critical platforms — not CRUD apps.

Ownership & accountability

You own your work end-to-end. We don't have layers of management reviewing every decision — we hire people we trust to make good calls independently.

Remote-first, async-friendly

Our team spans multiple time zones. We default to async communication and trust our people to manage their own time and output.

Continuous learning

Annual learning budget, conference attendance, and a culture of internal knowledge sharing. We stay current because the work demands it.

Fair compensation

We pay at or above market for every role. No lowball offers, no equity cliff manipulation — straightforward compensation with clear progression.
