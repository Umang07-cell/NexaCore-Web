Trust & Safety

Security at NexaCore

Security is a first-class engineering concern — not a compliance exercise. Here's how we protect our systems, our clients' data, and the platforms we build and operate.

Our Practices

Built secure from the ground up

Zero Trust Architecture

Every system we build operates on the principle that no network, device, or user is inherently trusted. Access is always verified, never assumed.

Secrets Management

We use HashiCorp Vault and equivalent solutions to manage secrets, credentials, and certificates — no hardcoded secrets, no shared passwords, ever.

Secure SDLC

Security requirements are captured during discovery. Threat modelling, SAST scanning, dependency audits, and security review are built into every delivery pipeline.

Continuous Monitoring

All production systems we operate run continuous vulnerability scanning, anomaly detection, and security event logging with automated alerting for critical findings.

Identity & Access Management

Role-based access control, MFA enforcement, privileged access management, and quarterly access reviews are standard on every client platform we administer.

Data Protection

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256 and TLS 1.3+. We apply data minimisation principles and enforce retention limits on all client engagements.

Vulnerability Disclosure

Responsible disclosure policy

Report a security issue

If you've discovered a security vulnerability in our website or systems, we want to hear about it. We take all reports seriously and will respond within 48 hours.

Please email your findings to security@nexacore.com. Include a description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce it, and the potential impact. We ask that you give us reasonable time to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.

We do not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith and follow responsible disclosure principles. We appreciate your help in keeping our systems secure.

Security questions for your procurement team?

We're happy to complete security questionnaires, provide documentation, or schedule a technical review call with your security team.
