Privacy Policy

We keep policies clear and practical. Here's what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Overview

NexaCore Technologies, Inc. ("NexaCore", "we", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our website and services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact form submissions (name, email address, company, message content)
  • Career applications (CV/resume, contact information, professional background)
  • Business correspondence and meeting notes from client engagements

We also collect limited technical information automatically, including IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited, for security and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests
  • Evaluate and communicate with job applicants
  • Deliver contracted services to clients
  • Maintain business records and legal compliance
  • Improve our website and service quality

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist in our operations (e.g. cloud infrastructure, email services), subject to confidentiality agreements. We will disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. Client data handled as part of service engagements is governed by the relevant Master Services Agreement and Data Processing Addendum.

Data Retention

We retain contact enquiry data for up to 24 months. Career application data is retained for 12 months. Client engagement data is retained per the terms of the applicable service agreement. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@nexacore.com.

Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics beyond standard server logs.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact our privacy team at privacy@nexacore.com or write to NexaCore Technologies, Inc., 580 Howard Street, San Francisco, CA 94105.
